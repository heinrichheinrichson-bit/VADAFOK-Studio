# Voice Quick Cards – 2.16.8 RC1C

RC1C improves voice usability without changing the Quick Card library.

- Fixes `Vadafok Stop` while the suggestion window is open.
- Adds `Vadafok Back` to return to Quick Caption without sending.
- Adds context-sensitive voice-command help to Quick Caption and the Quick Card suggestion window.
- The suggestion help lists only number commands that are currently available.
- Quick Cards remain read-only during voice matching.
