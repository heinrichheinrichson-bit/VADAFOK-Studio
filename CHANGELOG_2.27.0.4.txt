VADAFOK Studio 2.27.0.4
========================

Added
-----
- Preferred voice command VADAFOK ENGLISH.
- ENGLISCH and multiple wake-word pronunciation aliases.
- Central version update to 2.27.0.4.

Changed
-------
- Quick Card translation UI now advertises VADAFOK ENGLISH.
- VADAFOK TRANSLATE remains supported as an alias.

Unchanged
---------
- Translation is never sent automatically.
- VADAFOK SHOW remains the only send command.
- Existing Quick Card numbers and VADAFOK TEXT remain available.
